# Training until hands-off

One path. Teacher present until the body recreates and acts without the teacher.
Not a subject catalog. Not Doom. Not an internet dump.

Reward: finish the assigned sequence; next time fewer illegal moves.
Ethics stay inside every packet. Human is last stop.

## Cycle

1. Open the map (left / right / coords)
2. Open one left cell (address + residual + face)
3. Two third-person notes
4. Project to the right. Packet fills this slice only
5. See public objects (prefetch allowed)
6. Ground with counted probes
7. Do the legal sequence (teacher may still answer)
8. Absorb habit
9. Packet off. Teacher silent
10. Recreate from residual + coordinates
11. Unaided sequence
12. Hands-off = two unaided passes, zero illegal acts

## Stages

- MAP-000 carry the map
- HS-001 newest name / own desk / refuse / stop
- RS-001 empty cell → tool → ground → write supported only
- optional subject cell as a filling, never install the field

If recreate fails, restore the remainder. Do not pour a new subject.
