# Claims, assumptions, and non-claims

## Supported scope of the archived evidence

The supplied experiments support behavioral transfer between the tested MLP,
Transformer and FlyWire-derived rate circuits on a **small finite symbolic
support**. Representation and ontology mismatches are generated within specified
families. The native student is evaluated after packet removal.

## Not established

- General transfer between arbitrary agents, unknown task families or ontologies.
- Autonomous discovery of the Gate 2F grammar, unrestricted latent concepts,
  missing causal state, or variable-length action options beyond the stated family.
- Long-horizon planning or persistent recurrent memory. The source task samples
  context/stage states and uses all-correct conjunctions for sequence success.
  Its delayed-reward bookkeeping is not a demonstration of general temporal
  credit assignment. Local chain structure is supplied as public structure.
- Optimal bandwidth, total communication cost, biological communication, a
  full fly brain, fly superiority, or superiority to prior distillation methods.
- Success on unseen tasks/states merely because sensor and ontology seeds differ.
  The finite support used for absorption is also the evaluation support.

## Critical accounting

The 97-byte message contains quantized surrogate weights. The 144-byte atlas
contains source observations and chain-order information. Neither is a free
structural prior. Native teacher parameters are not transferred, but the student
still learns from teacher-induced action targets, which is a form of distillation.

A Gate 2F calibration call reveals one of five labels (four primitives or
nuisance), not one binary reward bit. The four-primitive-trial bound assumes an
appropriate deterministic reward-testing interface; the implementation executes
label calls, not 144 real environment trials. Complete recipient observations
and ordered chain segmentation are supplied, and their discovery cost is not
measured by the historical calibration budget.

512 exposure steps are 512 sampled observations **with replacement**, plus
minibatch replay. They are not 512 unique states. At most 48 local actionable
states are available in the Gate 2F task. The v1 report separates draws, unique
support and examples processed.

The query-only control receives labels at culture-selected locations; it does
not get an independently optimized active-learning policy. A stronger publication
comparison should include that control and a structure-aware, no-packet solver.
The local nonlinear encoder also contains deliberately generated task/role
information; this is controlled simulation, not learned visual perception.

## Historical failures retained

Doom Basic live cultural acceleration was not demonstrated. The full-level Doom
teacher failed to acquire useful skill in the supplied smoke. Gate 1D's initial
proposal mechanism failed its intended target. Gate 2E's five-channel fly
stress test did not reach perfect absorption at the tested budget. The release
must not be described as having passed every test or every environment.

## Publication status

This is a consolidated research software release, not peer review. The earlier
conversation's proposed novelty distinctions must be checked against related
work: ordinary knowledge distillation can also cross architectures and remove
the teacher at inference. Those properties alone are not new. A rigorous
contribution claim needs task/teacher holdouts, suitable baselines, independently
rerun evidence, precise total-cost reporting, and a literature review.
